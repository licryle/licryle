### Emerging Technologies
LLMs are amazing, and I love what can be done and automated, but I have 3 reservations:
- Currently, the market is all about hooking everyone with heavily subsidized tokens. I keep an eye on local models capabilities.
- Because "creating something" has become so cheap, I'm seeing a heavy *loss of focus* on what to build, everyone thinking they have the next idea powered by AI
- LLMs are non-deterministic, and as such, when I build a tool, I'd rather use LLM to build as much deterministic as possible, with LLM filling the voids where necessary.
